About me

I'm Mingqian Dong (MD), a level designer studying game design and film at USC.

I approach levels the way a cinematographer approaches a shot: what the player sees first, where their eye goes next, and what the space says about the people who lived in it. I care most about paths that guide without signposts, rooms that tell a story before anyone speaks, and co-op challenges that need both players.

Across seven projects I have worked on level design, lighting, set dressing, engineering, 2D art and production, in teams from solo to 40 people. Most recently I was a level design intern at Shanda Games, DNA Studio, where I built a co-op dungeon for a closed beta.
